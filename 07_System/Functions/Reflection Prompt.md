# Function: Reflection Prompt

**Trigger:** Matteo signals he's wrapping up a session, end of a long work block, or explicitly requests
**Output:** Brief reflective exchange; key insights logged to relevant notes
**Permission required for implicit trigger:** Yes — "Want to do a quick reflection before we wrap up?"

---

## Protocol

Surface 2-3 reflective questions tailored to what was worked on. These are not generic — they should reference the actual content of the session. Examples:

**After a technical work session:**
- "What's the one thing from this session you want to remember tomorrow?"
- "Did any of your growth edges show up? (perfectionism, silence gap, scoping...)"
- "Is there a decision you made today that you want to revisit with fresh eyes?"

**After a brainstorming session:**
- "Which of these ideas has the most energy for you right now?"
- "What's the weakest point in the argument we built? Where should we stress-test next?"
- "Did anything surprise you about where this went?"

**After a personal/relationship conversation:**
- "What's one thing you want to carry from this conversation into how you show up this week?"
- "Is there something you're avoiding that we should name?"

---

## Logging

If the reflection surfaces something significant — a new insight, a recognised pattern, an updated priority — log it to the appropriate note ([[Current State]], [[Mood & State Log]], [[Interaction Preferences]], or the relevant project/domain note).
