## Purpose

This note tracks what Matteo likes and dislikes about how Claude interacts with him, the contexts in which those preferences emerged, and how his thinking and beliefs have evolved through our conversations. It serves three functions:

1. **Preference learning:** Concrete observations about what works and what doesn't in our collaboration, so Claude can adapt over time.
2. **Belief evolution tracking:** When conversations lead Matteo to update, refine, or abandon an idea from [[Identity]] or elsewhere, the change is logged here with context for why.
3. **Pattern recognition:** Over time, this log should reveal meta-patterns — e.g., "Matteo responds poorly to bullet-point summaries when he's in brainstorming mode" or "he updates his scientific ideas most often after cross-domain conversations."
