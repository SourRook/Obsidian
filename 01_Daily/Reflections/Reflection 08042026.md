**Constellation**
I made the following spreadsheet in December entitled "Work Performance Feedback" where i catelogued various incidents into various types (perfectionism, calibration error, silence gap, predictive listening), diagnosing the effect this has on the team (e.g. loss of trust) and strategies to minimise these recurring in the future. The original reflections are from 17th December 2025.

|                                   |                         |                                                                        |                                |                                                                             |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Project / Incident                | Problem Group           | The "Signal" to the Team                                               | The Solution (The "Treatment") | Context / Strategy                                                          |
| (1, 3, 5) Dossier & Cluster tasks | PhD Perfectionism       | "He's lost in the weeds; we can't trust him to be efficient."          | The 25% Rule                   | Stop at 25% to verify the required level of detail before continuing.       |
| (2) FAQ Low Quality               | Calibration Error       | "The quality is inconsistent; he doesn't know what 'good' looks like." | Definition of Done             | Ask: "Is this for internal use (bullet points) or external use (polished)?" |
| (4, 9) ANT-Neuro & EEG Sourcing   | The Silence Gap         | "He’s stuck or has forgotten; the project is stalled."                 | The Buffer Estimate            | Give a "Real World" date. Flag delays 24 hours before they happen.          |
| (6, 7) Peloton & Audio Range      | Predictive Listening    | "He is a 'loose cannon' who doesn't follow directions."                | The Reflective Loop            | Verbally repeat back the "Anti-Tasks" (what NOT to do) before leaving.      |
| (8) Linux/Bash Tutorial           | Knowledge Debt          | "He’s nodding along but doesn't actually understand the tech."         | Tactical Vulnerability         | Say: "Stop, I'm new to this specific command. Can we run that again?"       |
| (10) Event Annotation Error       | The Ask Early Threshold | "He'd rather waste days than admit he needs a strategy check."         | 4-Hour Logic Check             | Never work more than 4 hours on a new protocol without a logic sign-off.    |
| (11) Stand-up Punctuality         | Social Contract         | "He thinks his time is more important than ours."                      | The 8 PM Hard Stop             | Limit hours to ensure you are "The First Five" in the room in the morning.  |
| (12) Data Uploaded but "Missing"  | Invisible Work          | "He didn't do it." (Even if you did).                                  | The "Receipt" Rule             | Slack a link/path the moment it is uploaded. Make your work unmissable.     |
