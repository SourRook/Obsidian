
- Parity
    
    - Debriefs every few weeks about how we feel about being open and any issues we have
    
    - Not hooking up with someone until there is parity in the rate of new partners/ number of times with a partner
    
    - No hooking up with multiple people at the same time or same night
    

- Respect
    
    - for each other’s time - meaning communicating when we need to reschedule, when we foresee a very busy week or day and won’t be able to communicate much
    
    - not dragging out fights as punishment (silent treatment)
    
    - getting tested every 3rd person or every 3rd month (whichever comes first)
    
    - wearing protection
    
    - checking in with ourselves if we are taking conversations to where the other person wants them to go (i.e. when to give advice, when to listen, when to challenge)
    

- Romance
    
    - calling at least once a week for proper catch up
    
    - texting each other every day
    
    - sharing when we miss each other
    
    - complimenting and flirting with each other
    
    - picking up shared hobbies so that we can enjoy them when we are together
    

- Honesty
    
    - telling each other the truth about what we feel
    
    - taking each other at face value and not assuming ulterior motives
    
    - checking in with each other explicitly about many things to facilitate conversations
