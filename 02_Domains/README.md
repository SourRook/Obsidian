Domains are ongoing parts of your life that do not end. Work, Relationships, Health, Creative Practice, Learning, Finance, and so on. These live in `02_Domains/`. Each domain gets a home note that defines its contents and remits, what relevant content should live here, what are the long-term goals and current priorities for this domain, and what projects (if any) belong to it. For you, I would likely start with `Work`, `Relationships`, `Health`, `Creative`, and `Learning`. For instance `Japanese` (ANKI decks, notes etc) sits naturally under `Learning` unless it becomes a dedicated project.
