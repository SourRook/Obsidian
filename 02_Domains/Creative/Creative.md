## Creative Identity

I grew up in a household of artists. My father (Raul Alejandro Vinao) is a composer; my mother (Lachele Jeana Carl) is an actress. I played piano from age four and held a music scholarship at City of London School for Boys. As a teenager I read voraciously — 200 pages a day as part of a pact with a small cabal of bookworms. The full canon: Joyce (Ulysses was my favourite novel), Dostoevsky, Tolstoy, Byron, T.S. Eliot, Tennyson, Robert Musil.

The arts are not separate from my scientific identity — they are foundational to it. My scientific thinking is shaped by narrative, aesthetics, and the same pattern-recognition that drives literary reading.
