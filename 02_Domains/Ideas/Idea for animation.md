There’s a dark room pitch black. Footprints appear on the floor illuminating the space around in a dim light. Each footstep illuminates the room in a brief flash of dim light. We see a black infinite empty space and a boy and girl walking through the darkness facing the camera. After few illuminations of the boy and girl we see that the space around their feet is grass (black and white) and there are white pulsating specs in the sky (stars)
