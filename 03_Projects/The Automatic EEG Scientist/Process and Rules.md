- 2. Daily Logs: This section tracks your work in reverse chronological order (newest entries at the top). The goal here is speed, not polish.
    
    - Format: Date + Subtitle + Short bulleted notes.
    - Meeting notes should include feedback + next actionable steps.
    - Visuals: Add any sketches (whiteboard), raw data plots, or generated figures immediately.
    - Literature: When linking papers, add a brief sentence describing why it is relevant to your specific context.
    - Experiments: Document the process rather than just the result:
        - Current Hypothesis: What are you testing?
        - Results: What did the data show?
        - Deviations: Why did it differ from expectations?
        - Next: Based on this result, what is the next logical step?
