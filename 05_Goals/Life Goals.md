## Cosmic-Scale Aspirations

These are the things that, if I achieved them across the arc of my life, would make me feel it was well-spent:

1. **Found a research lab** with a brother/sister PI studying whole-body embodied cognition using multi-modal imaging. Understand the mechanisms underpinning extreme states of consciousness — DMT, sleep, consciousness in children, insects, sense of self, the ego, Jhanas, meditative states — and what the critical elements are for these states to arise from the brain and body.

2. **Build intelligent systems from first principles.** Not just models that predict — systems that compute, that teach us something about how cognition actually works. Feynman's dictum: you cannot understand something until you have built it.

3. **Build a life with Yuval.** Move back to London, start a family, continue growing together in a relationship that is deep, honest, and expansive.

4. **Stay connected to the arts.** Never let the scientist fully consume the reader, the pianist, the person who fell in love with Ulysses at sixteen.

5. **Use AI to understand human cognition and consciousness** without conceding what it means to be human — our capacity to be creative and to think critically.
